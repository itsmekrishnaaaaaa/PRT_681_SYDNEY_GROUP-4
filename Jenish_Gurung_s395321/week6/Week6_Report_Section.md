# Project Report – Week 6 Update
**Report Writer: Jenish Gurung**

## Summary of Activities

This week the team progressed project management setup and architecture review for the OraEarn application.

**Project Board Setup:** The team established a Jira project (PRT681_S2026_Group_4) to manage the OraEarn backlog. Course facilitators (Nare and Anand) were added for oversight. The backlog was populated with epics covering user management, job and shift tracking, visa hour monitoring, expense and savings tracking, and reporting/analytics — derived directly from the existing SRS and BRD documentation.

**Architecture Review:** The team cloned and ran a sample 3-tier .NET application (WebApplication3tierApp) to study a reference implementation of presentation, business logic, and data access layers. This was compared against OraEarn's planned architecture and existing ERD, confirming that the current entity design (Application_User, Job, Shift, Active_Shift_Session, Expense_Category, Expense, Savings_Goal) aligns well with a standard 3-tier structure.

**Individual Research:** Team members completed research into two IT-related career roles relevant to their assigned project roles, including required skills, tools, and a structured learning path with milestones. This research was documented in individual OneNote pages.
